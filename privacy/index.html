Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

"Poems Companion" (古诗文伴读, the "App") respects and protects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user information.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It requires no account registration, has no login feature, uses no third-party analytics or advertising services, and does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other device permissions.

Data Storage

All poem content — including the original text, Chinese and English translations, and the background classical music audio files — is bundled with the App and requires no internet connection to use. Any in-app usage data (such as quiz scores, if applicable) is stored only locally on the user's device and is never uploaded to any server.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party data collection, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages, including children. Because the App collects no personal information whatsoever, there are no children's privacy risks associated with its use.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the latest version will be posted on this page. Users are encouraged to check back periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the support channel listed on the App Store product page.

隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026 年 8 月

《古诗文伴读》（以下简称"本应用"）尊重并保护用户的隐私。本隐私政策说明本应用如何处理用户信息。

信息收集

本应用不收集、不存储、不传输任何用户个人信息。本应用无需注册账号，不包含用户登录功能，不使用第三方分析或广告服务，不请求访问通讯录、位置、相机、麦克风等设备权限。

数据存储

本应用的所有内容——包括古诗文原文、中英文翻译，以及背景古典音乐音频文件——均随应用安装包一同提供，无需联网即可使用。应用内的测试成绩等使用记录（如有）仅保存在用户设备本地，不会上传至任何服务器。

第三方服务

本应用不集成任何第三方数据收集、统计分析或广告 SDK。

儿童隐私

本应用适合各年龄段用户使用，包括未成年人。由于本应用不收集任何个人信息，因此不存在未成年人个人信息保护相关风险。

政策变更

如本隐私政策有更新，我们会在本页面公布最新版本。建议用户定期查看本页面以了解最新政策内容。

联系方式

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 App Store 应用页面提供的支持渠道与我们联系。
