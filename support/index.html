DailyChinesePoems — Support & FAQ

Thank you for using DailyChinesePoems (每日古文). The app was originally built to help Chinese students study and self-test on the poems required by their school curriculum — it has since grown into a bilingual companion for classical Chinese poetry more broadly: a way for Chinese speakers to refresh poems they once memorized, and for English-speaking learners to explore Chinese poetry, language, and culture through full English translations. If you run into any issues or have suggestions, feel free to reach out.

FAQ

Q: Does the app require an internet connection?
A: No. All poem content, translations, and music are bundled with the app and work fully offline.

Q: Does the app include English translations?
A: Yes. Every poem includes the original Chinese text, a modern Chinese paraphrase (译文), and a full English translation — for the title, author, dynasty, and the poem itself. This makes the app useful both for Chinese speakers refreshing their memory and for English-speaking learners discovering Chinese poetry for the first time.

Q: What is the background music in the app?
A: The app plays traditional Chinese instrumental music in the background (guqin, guzheng, erhu, and other classical pieces). A track plays automatically when you open the app, and you can pick a specific piece from the music tab at any time.

Q: How are the quiz questions generated?
A: Each quiz session randomly draws 10 questions from a pool, and the questions themselves are dynamically generated from the poem content — so the question set varies each time.

Q: Who is the app for?
A: Anyone interested in classical Chinese poetry — students preparing for school exams (elementary through high school, based on China's national curriculum), adults who want to revisit poems they learned long ago, and English speakers learning Chinese language and culture.

Q: What content does the app cover?
A: The app currently includes 208 required poems and classical texts spanning elementary, middle, and high school (小学/初中/高中) curriculum levels, each with full bilingual content as described above.

Q: Does the app collect personal information?
A: No. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Feedback

《每日古文》支持与常见问题

常见问题

Q：应用需要联网才能使用吗？
A：不需要。所有古诗文内容、翻译和音乐均随应用一同安装，完全离线可用。

Q：应用是否提供英文翻译？
A：是的。每篇古诗文均包含原文、现代汉语译文（译文），以及完整的英文翻译——标题、作者、朝代和正文均有对应英文版本。这既方便中文用户温习巩固，也方便正在学习中文的英语使用者初步了解中国古典诗词。

Q：应用中的背景音乐是什么？
A：应用会播放中国古典器乐（古琴、古筝、二胡等传统乐曲）作为背景音乐。打开应用时会自动随机播放一首，您也可以随时在"音乐"页面中选择想听的曲目。

Q：测试模式的题目是如何生成的？
A：每次进入测试，系统会从题库中随机抽取 10 道题，题目本身也是根据古诗文内容动态生成的，因此每次测试的题目组合都不完全相同。

Q：这款应用适合哪些人使用？
A：适合所有对中国古典诗词感兴趣的人——包括正在备考的中小学生（涵盖小学至高中，依据国家课程标准整理）、希望重新温习旧时所学诗词的成年人，以及正在学习中文语言与文化的英语使用者。

Q：应用支持哪些学段的古诗文？
A：目前收录小学、初中、高中三个学段共 208 篇必背古诗词与文言文篇目，均已配有上述完整的中英双语内容。

Q：应用是否收集个人信息？
A：不收集。详见我们的隐私政策。
