FAQ

Q: Does the app require an internet connection?
A: No. All poem content is bundled with the app and works fully offline.

Q: How are the quiz questions generated?
A: Each quiz session randomly draws 10 questions from a pool, and the questions themselves are dynamically generated from the poem content — so the question set varies each time.

Q: What grade levels does the app cover?
A: The app currently includes 208 required poems and classical texts across elementary, middle, and high school (小学/初中/高中), based on China's national curriculum standards.

Q: Does the app collect personal information?
A: No. See our Privacy Policy for details.

常见问题

Q：应用需要联网才能使用吗？
A：不需要。所有古诗文内容均随应用一同安装，完全离线可用。

Q：测试模式的题目是如何生成的？
A：每次进入测试，系统会从题库中随机抽取 10 道题，题目本身也是根据古诗文内容动态生成的，因此每次测试的题目组合都不完全相同。

Q：应用支持哪些学段的古诗文？
A：目前收录小学、初中、高中三个学段共 208 篇必背古诗词与文言文篇目，均依据新课标要求整理。

Q：应用是否收集个人信息？
A：不收集。详见我们的隐私政策。
